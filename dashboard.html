CALM-Net

Calibrated, abstaining, longitudinal EEG decoder for closed-loop lower-limb exoskeleton control. NeuroRex dataset (OpenNeuro ds007788), 7 subjects × 9 sessions.
COMPLETE · invariance check 7 / 7 ✓

Headline finding

~0.70movement-invariant decode

The walk/stop label on this paradigm is the exoskeleton's motion. Once the decoder is forced to be movement-invariant, accuracy settles near 0.70 regardless of model or data — a hard, data-imposed ceiling. Any higher number is the movement confound leaking back in. The contribution is therefore a calibrated, abstaining, coverage-guaranteed framework, not an accuracy figure.
