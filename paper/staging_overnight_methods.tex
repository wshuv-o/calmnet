\textbf{Cohort C (motor execution, PhysioNet EEGMMIDB).} Twenty participants,
S001--S020, from the PhysioNet motor movement and imagery database
\citep{schalk2004,goldberger2000}: 64-channel EEG recorded at 160\,Hz and
resampled to 100\,Hz. Only the motor-execution runs are used, and the task is
rest against movement of the hands or feet. The model is fitted on runs 3, 5,
7 and 9 and evaluated on runs 11 and 13, with 2\,s windows at 0.5\,s stride,
each lying wholly inside one annotated period. The protocol alternates the two
classes about every 4\,s, which gives blocks far shorter than in either other
cohort. The cohort's analysis plan, including the predictions tested in
Section~\ref{sec:prereg}, was committed to version control before any model
was trained on it.

% ---------------------------------------------------------- Preprocessing
% insert after the preprocessing paragraph

\paragraph{Artefact control} To test whether decoding depends on non-neural
activity, the proposed configuration was retrained on cohort~A after
independent component analysis. Each recording was re-referenced to the
average, a copy band-passed to 1--45\,Hz was decomposed into 30 components with
the extended Picard algorithm \citep{picard}, and the components that ICLabel
\citep{iclabel} classified as eye, muscle, heart, line noise or channel noise
were removed before band-passing to the decoding band. Components classified as
brain or other were kept. Both arms of the comparison use the training task
only, so they differ in the cleaning step alone. Gait-related artefact is known
to be difficult to separate from cortical activity during walking
\citep{castermans2014,kline2015}, and ICLabel is not trained specifically on
it, so this control reduces rather than eliminates the question.

% ------------------------------------------------------ Statistical treatment
% insert at the end of the statistical treatment subsection

Where an arm was run with three seeds, each participant's accuracy is averaged
over the seeds before pairing, so that run-to-run variation does not enter the
test as independent samples. Alignment is then compared with an otherwise
identical arm without it, differing in that single factor, by a two-sided
Wilcoxon signed-rank test over participants. This is the same design on all
three cohorts.
